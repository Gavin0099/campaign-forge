# Field 24×18 art — PLACEHOLDER

Original deterministic brush/noise tiles (with an earth crop of our existing
`art/battlefield.png` for brush texture), river-bank/crossing chunks and separate
tree/rock/banner props made by `tools/make_field_art.py`, seed24018, Pillow.
No download, commercial asset, AI image or figure/frame editing. Muted earth,
olive foliage, slate water; light from upper left, same diamond perspective.
Programmatic painted approximation, **not final hand-painted art approval**.
Terrain semantics always come from `data/levels/field_24x18.gd`.

Characters are existing Thoma/knight stand-ins, ~96px reference height. Bow and
boot badges denote ranged/mobile TEST units, not canonical equipment/classes.
